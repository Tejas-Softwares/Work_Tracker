# Work Tracker: how we use it

From Monday 2 November, every piece of office work lives in the Work Tracker. If it isn't in the tracker, it wasn't assigned. This page is all you need.

## Every day

1. **Open My work** first thing. It lists your tasks: overdue first, then due today, this week, later.
2. **Update your tasks by 6 pm.** Tick the circle when something is done. Open a task to change its status, add a step, or post an update. A short update ("Quotes in from two vendors, third on Monday") is enough.
3. **Blocked?** Press **Blocked** and say what is blocking it. Whoever gave you the task and your manager are told, so someone can clear it.
4. **Need more time?** Change the due date and say why. The reason goes to whoever gave you the task and to your manager. Move a date early, not after it has passed.
5. **Daily huddle**, 15 minutes: the chair opens it from **Meetings**. It shows each person's late, due-today and blocked work. New work agreed there goes in as an action with an owner and a date.

## Adding a task in one line

Press **New task** (or the **+** on your phone, or the **N** key) and type:

`Send RA bill 7 to Jabil @Ravi #Jabil-1700 !high fri`

| Type | Means |
| --- | --- |
| `@name` | who owns it (first name is enough; if two people match, you choose) |
| `#tag` | the project or site |
| `!high`, `!critical`, `!!` | priority |
| `today`, `tomorrow`, `fri`, `25 Oct`, `25/10`, `in 3 days`, `eom` | due date |
| `every day`, `every monday`, `every month on 5th` | it repeats; a new task appears for each date |

Press Enter to add it. Everything it understood shows underneath, and you can change any of it before adding.

## Giving work to someone

Use `@name`. They get a notification, and it sits in their list with your name as the giver. It also shows under **Given by you** on your My work, so you can follow up without phone calls. Instructions given by phone or WhatsApp go in the same day, by whoever received them.

## On your phone

Open the address in Chrome and tap **Add to Home screen**. The bar at the bottom has My work, Tasks, **+**, Team and Meetings.

## Your role card and monthly score

**Team and roles**, then your name, shows your role card: why the role exists, your 4 to 6 KRAs with targets and weights, and expected behaviours. Each month your manager scores every KRA from 1 to 5 (70% of the score) and behaviours (30%), with the tracker's on-time figure beside it. You get a notification when it's ready; read it, add a comment if you want, and press **I've read my scorecard**.

| Score | Means |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | Not done |
| 2 | Partly met |
| 3 | Met the target |
| 4 | Above it |
| 5 | Well above |

Below 3 for two months running means a written 60-day improvement plan with weekly check-ins and support. It is a fair, recorded process, not a surprise.

## For managers

- **My work** shows your team's overdue, blocked and quiet people. **Dashboard** has the detail, including the muster roll of who logged work each day.
- A task overdue 2 working days comes to you automatically.
- Score your team by the 7th of each month under **Scorecards**. Hold a 20-minute one-to-one with each person monthly (start it from **Meetings**).
- Department heads enter their KPIs monthly under **Department KPIs**.

## Why we track work

So good work is visible, reviews are fair and based on facts, and nothing gets lost between a phone call and a site. Being busy and being productive look the same without a record; with one, they don't.
